## Understanding Greatest Common Factor (GCF)

The **Greatest Common Factor (GCF)** is the largest positive integer that divides each of the numbers in a given set without leaving a remainder. It is widely used in simplifying fractions, algebraic factoring, and number theory problems.

### Methods for Finding GCF

1. **Euclidean Algorithm**: An efficient division algorithm based on the principle that the greatest common divisor of two numbers also divides their difference.
2. **Prime Factorization**: Expressing each number as a product of primes and multiplying the smallest powers of common prime factors.
