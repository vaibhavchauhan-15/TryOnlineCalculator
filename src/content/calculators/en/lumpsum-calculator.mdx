## What is a Lumpsum Calculator?

A Lumpsum calculator estimates the future value of a one-time single investment. It illustrates the power of compounding over long investment horizons without recurring deposits.

## Key Benefits of Lump Sum Investing

1. **Full Market Exposure**: 100% of your capital starts compounding from day one.
2. **Exponential Growth**: Long investment horizons maximize compound interest gains.
3. **Low Maintenance**: Requires no ongoing monthly transaction management.
