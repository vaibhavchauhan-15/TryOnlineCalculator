## What is a Credit Card Payoff Calculator?

A Credit Card Payoff calculator maps out the path to becoming credit-card-debt-free. It projects payoff timelines and total interest expenses under different monthly payment amounts.

## Why Credit Card Debt Is Expensive

Credit card APRs frequently range between 18% and 29%. At these rates, paying only the minimum required payment can turn a modest debt into a lifetime financial burden. Increasing your monthly payment even slightly dramatically cuts interest fees.
