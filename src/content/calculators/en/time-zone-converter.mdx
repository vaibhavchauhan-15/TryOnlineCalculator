A time zone converter translates a time from one world time zone to another in
a single step. Select the source and target zones from 30+ options — including
UTC, EST, PST, IST, CET, JST, AEST and many more — enter the time in 24-hour
format, and see the result instantly.

The calculator handles half-hour and quarter-hour offsets (like India's UTC+5:30
and Nepal's UTC+5:45) and shows a "+1 day" or "−1 day" indicator when the
conversion crosses midnight. It also displays the total time difference between
the two zones in hours.

This is ideal for scheduling international calls, planning travel, coordinating
remote teams, and converting meeting times across regions.
