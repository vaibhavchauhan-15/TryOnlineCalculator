A commission calculator works out how much you earn on a sale and, if you have
a base salary, your total pay for the period. Enter the sale amount, your
commission rate and any base pay, and the commission and total earnings appear
instantly.

It is useful for sales roles, real-estate agents and freelancers who price by
percentage. For tiered or capped plans, run each band through the calculator
separately and add the pieces to build up your full payout.
