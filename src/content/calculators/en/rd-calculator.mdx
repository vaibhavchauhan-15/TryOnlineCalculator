## What is an RD Calculator?

A Recurring Deposit (RD) calculator is a tool designed to estimate the maturity value of monthly savings deposited in a recurring deposit scheme. It helps disciplined savers plan short to medium-term financial goals by forecasting total savings and interest returns.

## Why Choose a Recurring Deposit?

1. **Disciplined Savings**: Build a habit of investing a fixed sum every month.
2. **Guaranteed Returns**: Unlike stocks or mutual funds, RDs provide fixed, risk-free interest rates.
3. **Flexible Options**: Tenure options typically range from 6 months up to 10 years.
