## What is an NPS Calculator?

An NPS calculator calculates the retirement corpus, lump sum tax-free payout, and expected monthly pension generated by National Pension System investments. It helps individuals structure an effective pension strategy.

## Key Benefits of NPS

1. **Tax Benefits**: Extra tax deductions under specialized pension tax provisions.
2. **Low Cost**: Unmatched low asset management fees compared to mutual funds.
3. **Flexible Asset Allocation**: Choose between Auto Choice (age-based) and Active Choice (equity, corporate bonds, govt securities).
