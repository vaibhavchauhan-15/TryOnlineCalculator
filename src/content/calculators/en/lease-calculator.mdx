## What is a Lease Calculator?

A Lease calculator projects monthly payments when leasing a vehicle or equipment. It separates monthly depreciation costs from finance fees to reveal where your lease dollars go.

## Anatomy of a Monthly Lease Payment

1. **Depreciation Charge**: Pays for the value lost by the car during your lease term.
2. **Finance Fee (Rent Charge)**: Pays interest to the leasing company on the asset tied up.
3. **Sales Tax**: State and local taxes applied onto monthly payments in most jurisdictions.
