## What is a Down Payment Calculator?

A Down Payment calculator creates a roadmap to homeownership. It calculates your required down payment amount and projects how many months of savings are needed to buy a house.

## How to Accelerate Your Down Payment Savings

1. **High-Yield Savings Accounts (HYSA)**: Earn top market interest rates on cash reserves.
2. **Automate Transfers**: Schedule monthly transfers right when paychecks deposit.
3. **Cut Discretionary Expenses**: Temporarily trim non-essential spending to boost monthly contributions.
