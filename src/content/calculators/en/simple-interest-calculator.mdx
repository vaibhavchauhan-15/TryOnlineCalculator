## What is a Simple Interest Calculator?

A Simple Interest calculator quickly determines the interest charge on a loan or total returns on a simple interest deposit. Unlike compound interest, simple interest grows linearly over time.

## Simple Interest vs Compound Interest

- **Simple Interest (I = P × r × t)**: Interest earned each year is constant.
- **Compound Interest (A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n × t))**: Interest earns interest, causing exponential growth.
