## A simple calculator that works everywhere

This free online calculator handles the everyday math you reach for most: adding up a bill, splitting a total, working out a quick percentage or checking a number before you commit to it. It runs as a full calculator app right inside your browser tab, on phones and desktops alike, with large, easy-to-tap keys and a clear read-out.

## Type, tap or paste

You can enter a calculation three ways: press the on-screen keypad, use your physical keyboard (the number row, `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`, `^` and `Enter` all work), or paste a whole expression copied from somewhere else. Tap any value already on screen to select and edit just that number, so fixing a typo never means starting over.

## Order of operations, handled for you

The calculator follows standard PEMDAS/BODMAS precedence, so multiplication and division are resolved before addition and subtraction. Use parentheses to group terms and control exactly how an expression is evaluated — for example `(45 + 15) × 3` gives a different answer than `45 + 15 × 3`.

## Percentages and powers

The percent key turns the number before it into a fraction of one hundred, so `50%` becomes `0.5` and `200 × 15%` gives `30`. Raise a number to a power with the caret, for example `2^10` for two to the tenth.

## Private by default

Every calculation happens locally in your browser. Your numbers are never sent to a server, there is no sign-up, and your recent results stay on your own device so you can copy any answer with a single tap.
