## What is an Amortization Calculator?

An Amortization calculator creates a schedule tracking how your debt is paid off month by month. It details the precise breakdown of interest versus principal for every installment.

## Benefits of Reviewing Your Amortization Schedule

1. **Visualize Debt Decline**: Watch your principal balance decrease over time.
2. **Evaluate Refinancing**: Compare interest savings of switching terms or rates.
3. **Plan Prepayments**: See how extra principal payments shorten loan duration.
