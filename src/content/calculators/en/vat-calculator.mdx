A VAT (value added tax) calculator lets you add tax to a net price or strip it
out of a gross price in one step. Enter the amount, choose whether you are
working from a net or gross figure, set the rate, and the net price, VAT amount
and gross total appear instantly.

It is handy for invoices, quotes and expense checks. Adding VAT multiplies the
net price by one plus the rate; removing VAT divides the gross price by the same
factor to recover the original net amount and reveal the tax portion.
