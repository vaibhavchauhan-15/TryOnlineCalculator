A profit calculator shows how much money a sale or a whole business keeps after
costs. Enter your total revenue and total cost and it returns the net profit in
money, the profit margin as a percentage of revenue, and the markup on cost.

Use it to sanity-check pricing, compare products, or forecast the profit from a
deal. When cost climbs above revenue the result turns negative, flagging a loss
so you can adjust price or trim costs before you commit.
