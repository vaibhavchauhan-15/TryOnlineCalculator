A GST (Goods and Services Tax) calculator lets you add tax to a net price or
strip it out of a GST-inclusive price in one step. Enter the amount, choose
whether you are working from an exclusive or inclusive figure, set the rate, and
the net price, GST amount and gross total appear instantly.

GST is used in many countries around the world — India charges rates of 5%, 12%,
18% and 28%; Australia uses a flat 10%; Singapore levies 9%. In India,
intra-state GST is split equally into CGST (Central) and SGST (State), while
inter-state transactions attract IGST at the full rate. This calculator shows
the CGST/SGST split automatically.

It is handy for invoices, quotations, expense reports and tax filing. Adding GST
multiplies the net price by one plus the rate; removing GST divides the gross
price by the same factor to recover the original net amount and reveal the tax
portion.
