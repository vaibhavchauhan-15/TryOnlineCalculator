## What is a Personal Loan Calculator?

A Personal Loan calculator estimates your monthly installment (EMI) and total loan cost for unsecured borrowing. It incorporates origination fees to reveal the true cost of credit.

## Factors Affecting Personal Loan Rates

- **Credit Score**: Higher credit scores unlock lower interest rates.
- **Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio**: Lenders evaluate existing monthly debt obligations against income.
- **Loan Term**: Shorter loan terms decrease total interest paid despite slightly higher EMIs.
