## What is a Debt Payoff Calculator?

A Debt Payoff calculator demonstrates the mathematical power of extra monthly payments. It calculates the exact amount of money and time saved when you contribute more than the minimum required monthly payment.

## Strategies to Eliminate Debt Faster

1. **Debt Avalanche**: Target high-interest loans first to maximize dollar savings.
2. **Debt Snowball**: Target low-balance debts first to build momentum.
3. **Automate Extra Payments**: Set up automatic recurring extra transfers right after payday.
