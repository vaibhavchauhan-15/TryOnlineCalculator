## What is an SWP Calculator?

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) calculator models regular income payouts from a mutual fund or investment portfolio. It computes how much cash you can extract monthly without prematurely depleting your capital.

## Benefits of an SWP Strategy

1. **Regular Cash Flow**: Generate consistent monthly income for living expenses or retirement.
2. **Capital Growth**: Unwithdrawn funds remain invested and compound over time.
3. **Tax Efficiency**: Taxation applies only to gains, preserving your principal base.
