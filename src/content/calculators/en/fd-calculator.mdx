## What is an FD Calculator?

A Fixed Deposit (FD) calculator is a tool designed to calculate the maturity amount and interest earned on money deposited in a fixed deposit scheme. Fixed deposits are popular investment choices because they offer low risk and guaranteed returns compared to market-linked instruments.

## How Compounding Frequency Affects FD Returns

The frequency at which interest compounds plays a vital role in determining your final maturity amount:
- **Quarterly Compounding**: Interest is calculated every 3 months. This is the industry standard for fixed deposits.
- **Monthly Compounding**: Ideal for investors seeking monthly income payouts or higher effective annual yields.
- **Yearly Compounding**: Interest is added to the principal once every 12 months.

## Benefits of Using an FD Calculator

1. **Instant Accuracy**: Eliminates manual compounding errors.
2. **Comparison**: Easily compare different interest rates and tenures across banks.
3. **Financial Planning**: Plan long-term savings goals like home down payments, education funds, or emergency reserves.
