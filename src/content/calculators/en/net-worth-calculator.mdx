## What is a Net Worth Calculator?

A Net Worth calculator sums up your financial life into a single benchmark figure. It evaluates your total wealth by balancing what you own against what you owe.

## Categories of Assets and Liabilities

### Assets (What You Own)
- **Liquid Assets**: Cash, checking, savings, money market accounts.
- **Investments**: Stocks, bonds, mutual funds, 401(k), IRAs, crypto.
- **Physical Assets**: Primary home, real estate property, vehicles, jewelry, art.

### Liabilities (What You Owe)
- **Real Estate Loans**: Mortgages, home equity loans.
- **Consumer Debt**: Credit card balances, personal loans, medical debt.
- **Vehicle & Student Loans**: Auto loans, student loan balances.
