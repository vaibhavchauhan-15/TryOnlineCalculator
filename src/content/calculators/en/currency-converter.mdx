This currency converter runs entirely in your browser and uses live European
Central Bank reference rates, so conversions are fast, private and always
up to date on working days. Enter an amount, pick your two currencies, and the
result updates as you type — then dig into the history chart to see how the
pair has moved over the last week, month or several years.
