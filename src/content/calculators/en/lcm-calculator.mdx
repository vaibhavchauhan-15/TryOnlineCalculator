## Understanding Least Common Multiple (LCM)

The **Least Common Multiple (LCM)** of a set of integers is the smallest positive integer that is a multiple of all numbers in the set.

### How to Calculate LCM

- **Using GCF**: For two numbers a and b, `LCM(a,b) = (a × b) / GCF(a,b)`.
- **Prime Factorization**: Factorize each number into prime factors and take the maximum power of every prime factor present across the numbers.
