A countdown calculator tells you exactly how much time remains until a future
date. Enter any date — a holiday, deadline, birthday, wedding or milestone — and
see the remaining time broken down into days, weeks, months and a detailed
year-month-day format.

If the date has already passed, the calculator shows how long ago it was, so it
works as both a countdown and a "how long ago" tool. The total hours and
approximate total months are shown in the breakdown for extra detail.

Use it for tracking project deadlines, counting down to vacations, planning
event timelines, or simply answering the question "how many days until…?"
