## Understanding Exponents and Powers

Exponentiation is a mathematical operation involving two numbers: the **base** (x) and the **exponent** (y). It is written as x^y. When y is a positive integer, exponentiation corresponds to repeated multiplication of the base.

### Key Rules of Exponents

- **Zero Exponent**: `x^0 = 1` (for x ≠ 0)
- **Negative Exponent**: `x^(-y) = 1 / (x^y)`
- **Fractional Exponent**: `x^(1/y) = ʸ√x`
- **Product Rule**: `x^a · x^b = x^(a+b)`
- **Quotient Rule**: `x^a / x^b = x^(a-b)`
