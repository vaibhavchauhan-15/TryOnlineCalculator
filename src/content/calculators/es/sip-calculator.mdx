## What is a SIP Calculadora?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a way of investing a fixed Importe into mutual funds at regular intervals — usually every month, though Semanal and quarterly options also exist. It is one of two common ways to invest in mutual funds, the other being a one-time lumpsum.

A SIP Calculadora is a simple online tool that estimates the maturity value and returns of your Mensual investments. You enter how much you invest each month, the number of Años you stay invested, and an expected Anual Tasa of return, and it projects the corpus you could build.

The figures are estimates. Actual mutual fund returns vary with market conditions, and the Calculadora does not account for exit loads or a scheme's expense ratio.

## How can a SIP return Calculadora help you?

Investing through a SIP encourages financial discipline and a regular Ahorros habit, and it spreads your entry across market highs and lows (rupee-cost averaging). A Calculadora makes planning easier by showing what those regular investments could grow into.

In particular, it helps you decide how much to invest each month, tracks the Total you will have contributed over the tenure, and gives an estimate of the returns and final value — in seconds and without manual maths.

## How does this SIP Calculadora work?

The maturity value of a SIP is calculated with the standard future-value-of-an-annuity formula:

<strong>M = P × ((1 + i)<sup>n</sup> − 1) / i) × (1 + i)</strong>

where <strong>M</strong> is the maturity Importe, <strong>P</strong> is the Importe invested each month, <strong>n</strong> is the number of instalments, and <strong>i</strong> is the periodic (Mensual) Tasa of return.

The important detail is how <strong>i</strong> is derived. A common mistake is to divide the Anual return by 12 — treating a 12% Anual return as 1% a month. Because returns compound, that overstates the result: 1% compounded for 12 months works out to more than 12% a year.

The correct approach is to convert the Anual return into an effective Mensual return using <code>i = (1 + Anual return)<sup>1/12</sup> − 1</code>. For a 12% Anual return this comes to about 0.95% a month, not 1%, because compounding 0.95% over 12 months returns exactly 12%.

For example, investing ₹1,000 a month for 12 months at 12% a year uses i ≈ 0.0095, giving M = 1,000 × ((1.0095<sup>12</sup> − 1) / 0.0095) × 1.0095 ≈ <strong>₹12,766</strong>. This Calculadora uses that effective Mensual Tasa, so its maturity values line up with leading SIP tools.

Remember that the return Tasa is only an assumption. Real returns rise and fall with the market, so treat the output as a projection rather than a guarantee.

## Advantages of this SIP Calculadora

You can plan investments around any Importe and tenure, see an estimate of your Total corpus at the end of the SIP, and switch to lumpsum mode for one-time investments. An optional Anual step-up lets you raise your Mensual Importe each year to keep pace with a rising Ingresos, giving a realistic picture of a growing SIP.
