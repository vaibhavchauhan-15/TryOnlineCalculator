A mortgage payment is more than Capital and Interés: property Impuesto and home
insurance are usually rolled in too, which is why the "PITI" Total this
Calculadora shows is closer to what you actually pay each month. Adjust the home
Precio, down payment, Tasa and term to compare scenarios, and watch how a lower
Tasa or a bigger down payment cuts the Total Interés over the life of the loan.
