Body Mass Index is the most widely used way to check whether your Peso sits
in a healthy range for your Altura. This Calculadora goes a step further than a
plain BMI number: by adding your Edad and sex it also estimates your body-fat
percentage and shows exactly where you fall on the healthy-weight scale.

Use it as a quick screen, not a diagnosis — muscle, frame size and Edad all
affect what a given number means for you.
