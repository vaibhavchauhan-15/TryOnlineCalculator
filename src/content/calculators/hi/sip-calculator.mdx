## What is a SIP कैलकुलेटर?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a way of investing a fixed राशि into mutual funds at regular intervals — usually every month, though साप्ताहिक and quarterly options also exist. It is one of two common ways to invest in mutual funds, the other being a one-time lumpsum.

A SIP कैलकुलेटर is a simple online tool that estimates the maturity value and returns of your मासिक investments. You enter how much you invest each month, the number of वर्ष you stay invested, and an expected वार्षिक rate of return, and it projects the corpus you could build.

The figures are estimates. Actual mutual fund returns vary with market conditions, and the कैलकुलेटर does not account for exit loads or a scheme's expense ratio.

## How can a SIP return कैलकुलेटर help you?

Investing through a SIP encourages financial discipline and a regular बचत habit, and it spreads your entry across market highs and lows (rupee-cost averaging). A कैलकुलेटर makes planning easier by showing what those regular investments could grow into.

In particular, it helps you decide how much to invest each month, tracks the कुल you will have contributed over the tenure, and gives an estimate of the returns and final value — in seconds and without manual maths.

## How does this SIP कैलकुलेटर work?

The maturity value of a SIP is calculated with the standard future-value-of-an-annuity formula:

<strong>M = P × ((1 + i)<sup>n</sup> − 1) / i) × (1 + i)</strong>

where <strong>M</strong> is the maturity राशि, <strong>P</strong> is the राशि invested each month, <strong>n</strong> is the number of instalments, and <strong>i</strong> is the periodic (मासिक) rate of return.

The important detail is how <strong>i</strong> is derived. A common mistake is to divide the वार्षिक return by 12 — treating a 12% वार्षिक return as 1% a month. Because returns compound, that overstates the result: 1% compounded for 12 months works out to more than 12% a year.

The correct approach is to convert the वार्षिक return into an effective मासिक return using <code>i = (1 + वार्षिक return)<sup>1/12</sup> − 1</code>. For a 12% वार्षिक return this comes to about 0.95% a month, not 1%, because compounding 0.95% over 12 months returns exactly 12%.

For example, investing ₹1,000 a month for 12 months at 12% a year uses i ≈ 0.0095, giving M = 1,000 × ((1.0095<sup>12</sup> − 1) / 0.0095) × 1.0095 ≈ <strong>₹12,766</strong>. This कैलकुलेटर uses that effective मासिक rate, so its maturity values line up with leading SIP tools.

Remember that the return rate is only an assumption. Real returns rise and fall with the market, so treat the output as a projection rather than a guarantee.
